Our story

Serious about clean. Fun to work with.

Sparkle Mafia Cleaning Services is based at Kyle Crescent in Sheffield (S5). We clean offices, commercial premises, factories and warehouses across the city.

We think professional cleaning should be reliable, thorough and easy to arrange. That’s why we’re open 24 hours: so we can work around your business rather than the other way round. The name? We take cleaning seriously (that’s the “mafia” part) and we bring the sparkle to every job.

Whether you need a nightly office clean, a factory floor kept safe and tidy, or a one-off deep clean before an inspection, you’ll get the same care and attention to detail.
